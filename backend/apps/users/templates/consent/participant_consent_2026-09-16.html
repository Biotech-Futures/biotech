If media consent is provided, it may later be withdrawn by contacting {{ support_email }}. If media consent is withdrawn, the participant will no longer be permitted to attend in-person {{ brand_name }} events from the date the withdrawal takes effect.

{{ brand_name }} will take reasonable steps to stop future use and remove public media under its control following withdrawal. It may not be possible to remove material already printed, archived, cached, reposted by another person or included in a completed publication.
